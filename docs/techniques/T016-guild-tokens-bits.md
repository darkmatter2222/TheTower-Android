# T016 — Guild: the 33× token rule and the free contribution

Rev: 1 (2026-11-01) · Currency: **Guild Tokens**, **Bits**, **Coins**, **Gems**, **Module Shards**, plus Guardian **Chips**
**Value:** ★★★★ — the same weekly-box claim that T004/T013 already pay for also feeds the guild chests, and spending Tokens in the Guild Store is worth **15–30 gem-equivalents per token** while letting them auto-cash out pays **0.2**.
**Confidence:** **Medium-High** — unlock gate, contribution numbers, chest contents, the 5:1 auto-cashout and the shop quantities are fandom raw wikitext; the per-token efficiency table is derived from those numbers.
**Ease:** 1 tap per chest, 1 tap per purchase · **Prerequisite:** **Milestone Tier 3 Wave 10** (creating your own guild costs **50 gems**; joining is free).

---

## 1. Why this is nearly free income

- Joining a guild is free (search by **name** or **Guild ID** in the guild list, or accept an invite on the **Find** screen). Creating one costs **50 gems**; a guild holds up to **30** players.
- Your **contribution** comes entirely from the **Weekly Challenge boxes** you already claim for [T004](T004-mission-farming-low-tier.md) / [T013](T013-event-medal-economy.md): **each claimed box adds 5**, so one member contributes up to **35 per week** (7 boxes). The guild total resets **Monday 00:00 UTC**.
- At guild totals of **100 / 250 / 500 / 750** every member claims **their own** chest; unclaimed chests are **mailed** after the week ends, so a missed claim is not lost — but claiming on time keeps the tokens from piling against the season cut-off.

| Guild contribution | Tokens | Bits | Coins | Gems |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 10 | 10 | 1× | 5 |
| 250 | 20 | 25 | 2× | 10 |
| 500 | 40 | 50 | 3× | 15 |
| 750 | 80 | 100 | 5× | 30 |
| **Total / week** | **150** | **185** | **11×** | **60** |

The coin reward scales with your **highest unlocked tier** — identical to the Daily-Mission coin reward up to
Tier 18 (500 M) and flat at 500 M above it, so the “11×” above means **eleven copies of that tier's
daily-mission coin payout** per week. **Per 8-week season:** ≈ **1 200 Tokens, 1 480 Bits, 480 gems, 88× coins**.

## 2. The 33× rule: never let Tokens auto-cash out

Unspent **Guild Tokens are auto-cashed at the end of each Season (8 weeks) at 5 Tokens = 1 Gem** (rounded up,
mailed). The Guild Store pays far more per token:

| Purchase (per season, price rises per purchase count) | Price (Tokens) | You get | **Gem-equivalents per token** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shards** #1 / #2 / #3 (per module type) | 5 / 10 / 15 | **250 shards** each | 150 → 75 → 50 |
| **Gems** #1 / #2 / #3 | 15 / 25 / 40 | **100 gems** each | 6.7 → 4.0 → 2.5 |
| **Bits** #1 / #2 / #3 | 25 / 50 / 75 | Bits (for chip upgrades) | — |
| **Relics** | 75 or 150 | a limited relic | — |
| **Chips** (Ally/Attack come with the Guardian; others here) | **200** | one chip | — |
| Themes: tower / background / menu | 100 each | cosmetic **+ permanent coin bonus** | see §4 |
| Guardian skin | 150 | cosmetic + coin bonus | — |
| **Auto-cashout (do nothing)** | 5 : 1 | 1 gem per 5 tokens | **0.2** |

Using the repository's rates (1 shard = 0.6 gems, 1 stone = 10 gems — [T014 §2](T014-tournament-tickets.md)):
**shards = 30 gem-equivalents for 5 tokens (150/token), gems = 100 for 15 (6.7/token), auto-cashout = 0.2.**
That is **150× / 33×** better than the “do nothing” default. With 1 200 tokens/season, the first three shard
purchases plus the three gem purchases cost **30 + 80 = 110 tokens** and return **750 shards + 300 gems**; the
remaining tokens then go to chips/relics/themes — never to the cash-out.

*Shard purchases are per module type*, so Cannon/Armor/Generator/Core each have their own 5/10/15 ladder: four
chances at the cheap 5-token price.

## 3. Bits and the Guardian (why Bits are never wasted)

- The **Guardian** is unlocked for **200 Bits** and **only fights while you are in a guild** (chips and levels are
  kept if you leave; it just stops fighting).
- Each chip has **three stats** upgraded with Bits; **maxing one chip costs ≈ 13 000–14 500 Bits**, so Bits are a
  long-term sink — 185/week is ~1 480/season, i.e. one chip every ~9 season-weeks of chest claims.
- **Buy chips before levelling them**: chips 1–2 (Ally, Attack) come free with the Guardian; every other chip is
  **200 Tokens** in the store. The currency-relevant ones:
  - **Fetch** — “Find Chance 10 % (+2 % per level)”: the Guardian retrieves **coins, gems, medals, shards and
    modules**; worth **up to 10 Medals/day** (T013 §1) on top of the rest.
  - **Bounty** — non-common enemies near the tower grant **more coins on death** (×1.01 base, per-target).
  - **Summon** — spawns extra enemies and applies the **Cash Bonus** to all enemies while active (cash engine).
  - **Scout** / **Attack** — damage-shaping (range bonus), useful for pushing rather than for currency.
- **Bits never expire**, so hoarding Bits is safe; hoarding **Tokens** is only safe inside a season.

## 4. Cosmetics are a permanent coin multiplier

Every owned cosmetic adds a permanent coin bonus: **+0.4 % per tower skin, +0.8 % per background, +0.6 % per
menu / profile banner / Guardian skin.** A tower + background + menu bought for 300 tokens is **+1.8 % coins
forever**, which is why they rank above the auto-cashout but below shards/gems in the token queue.

## 5. Steps (UI only)

1. Home screen → **Guilds** panel (unlocks at Tier 3 Wave 10). Search by name/ID and join any **active** guild —
   the chests are keyed to the **guild's total** contribution, so an active guild reaches 750 and a dead one
   reaches 100.
2. Claim every **Weekly Challenge** box as it completes (each is worth **5** contribution *and* its own medals).
3. Claim the four **contribution chests** the moment they light up; if the week is about to roll, claim them
   before the **Monday 00:00 UTC** reset (anything unclaimed is mailed anyway, but mailed rewards still count
   toward the season's token cap on your spend window).
4. Open the **Guild Store** → **Currencies**: buy the **Shards** purchase for your build's module type at
   **5 tokens** (then 10, then 15), then the **Gems** purchases (15 / 25 / 40), then **Bits**.
5. Spend remaining tokens: **Chips (200)** → **Relics (75/150)** → **Themes (100/100/100, Guardian 150)**.
6. **Unlocks:** spend **200 Bits** on the Guardian (it must be unlocked before chips can be bought), then level
   **Fetch** first (it is the only chip that pays in five different currencies).
7. Repeat 2–4 weekly; the store prices **reset at the start of each season**, so do not save tokens past the
   8-week mark.

## 6. Reproduction checklist

- [ ] Claiming one Weekly Challenge box raises your guild contribution by exactly **5**.
- [ ] The four chest rows read **10/20/40/80** tokens and **10/25/50/100** bits at 100/250/500/750.
- [ ] The store's first **Shards** purchase costs **5 tokens for 250 shards**, the second **10**.
- [ ] The first **Gems** purchase costs **15 tokens for 100 gems**.
- [ ] The Guardian unlock costs **200 Bits**, and the Guardian stops fighting if you leave the guild.
- [ ] At the season end, leftover tokens arrive by mail as **⌈tokens ÷ 5⌉** gems.

## 7. Falsification

- If a weekly box raises contribution by something other than **5**, recompute the 35/week cap and the
  1 200-token season figure.
- If the first shard purchase does not give **250** shards, note the real quantity — the whole per-token table
  in §2 rescales.
- If unclaimed contribution chests are **not** mailed at week end, claiming before Monday 00:00 UTC becomes a
  hard deadline.
- If cosmetic purchases do not add a coin percentage, §4's ranking changes (they drop below chips entirely).
