# T022 — The full free-currency census + the paid ladder (web store vs in-app)

Rev: 1 (2026-11-01) · Currency: **Gems** and **Power Stones** (the two that gate everything), plus the spend-side price
census for both.
**Value:** ★★★★ — this is the *budget sheet*: the free flow is ≈ **360–435 gems/day** and **≈ 4 400 (Copper) / 10 700
(Gold) stones/year**, while the published bills are **≈ 115 000 gems** (all card slots + all cards + lab slots) and
**≈ 60 000 stones** (9 UWs + UW+ + card masteries + assist slots + Bot+ + Synchronicity). Knowing both sides is what
makes the spend order in T009/T012/T017 actually executable.
**Confidence:** **High** for the per-source amounts and every listed price (fandom `Currency/Gems` and
`Currency/Power_Stones` raw wikitext, which are transcribed from the live UI; the official store confirms the
**+10 % web-store gem bonus**), **Medium** for the yearly extrapolation (assumes a stable league + perfect claiming) and
for the web-store bonus on stones (H030). **Unverified** on device.
**Ease:** 1–2 taps per source per day + 1 web-store login/week · **Prerequisite:** none (Bob needs wave 10, the web
store needs your **User ID** from the profile screen).

## 1. The free income census (nothing here costs money)

**Gems — every published free source, with its real limit:**

| Source | Amount | Limit / mechanic (as published) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ad gems** (bottom-left icon) | **6** per claim | ≈1 new claim per **10 min** of play, **≤ 60 claims/day = 360 gems** (T007) |
| **Store → Free Daily Gems** | **20** | once/day, **doubled (40) during holiday sales** |
| **Web-store Weekly Free Gift** | (weekly) | claim by logging in with your **User ID** — no purchase needed (H031) |
| **Floating gems (“Bob”)** | **2** each, **≤ 10 per run** | first from **wave 10** if ≥ **11 min** since your last tap; each next needs **≥ 30 min and ≥ 400 waves** |
| **Daily Missions** | **3** each tile | 3–5 tiles/day (T004); rerolls cost **5** gems each |
| **Weekly Reward boxes** (Missions) | **185 gems/week** | plus **stones** in boxes 5/6/7 = 10/15/20 = **45/week** |
| **Tournament** | **100 → 900** (1st, Copper → Mythic) | 2 brackets/week; lower ranks less (T014's full table) |
| **Guardian `Fetch` ability** | **≤ 20 gems/day** | needs the Guardian chip (T015/T016) |
| **Guild weekly chest + leftover tokens** | season-dependent | tokens convert **1 gem per 5 tokens** at season end |
| **Medal trades (Event Shop)** | **150 gems** per trade line | 40-medal first trade (T013); stones **15** per trade |
| **Milestones** | 4 675 gems free through T12 | one-time (T019) |
| **One-time** | 40 (first Cards open) + 20 (link account) | — |
| **Offer walls (TapJoy)** | varies | extra rewarded offers inside the store panel |

**Bob, the timer detail that changes the routine:** because each block needs *both* ≥30 min *and* ≥400 waves since the
previous tap, the 10-block cap is only reachable in a **long single run** (10 × 30 min ≈ 5 h). With `Auto Restart`
(Vault) the run counter resets, so the practical rhythm is **≈ 1 block / 11 min early, then 1 per 30 min** ⇒ a 8-h
night of autoplay ≈ **16–20 gems** — 2 taps per hour at most.

**Daily/weekly budget (sub-T11, no purchases, 8 h uptime):**
`288 (48 ad claims) + 20 (store) + 12 (4 mission tiles) + 20 (Bob) + 20 (Fetch) ≈ 360 gems/day`;
24-h uptime reaches the **360**-gem ad cap ⇒ **≈ 435 gems/day**. Weekly, on top: **+185** boxes **+ 200–1 800**
tournament (two brackets, 1st place 100 → 900 by league).
⇒ **≈ 130 k–170 k gems/year.**

**Stone budget:** `45/week` from the weekly boxes = **2 340/year**, plus tournament 1st place **20 (Copper) → 475
(Mythic)** per bracket, ×2/week ⇒ **≈ 4 400 stones/year** in Copper, **≈ 10 700** in Gold (80 × 2 × 52 = 8 320). Medal
trades add **15** stones per trade line but cost the 150-gem alternative (T013's split).

## 2. The spend census (what the gems/stones are actually owed for)

**Gems (published prices):** Card slots **50 … 10 000** (**58 400** for slots 2–22, +6 more from the Vault = 28 slots);
Lab slots **100 / 400 / 1 400 / 3 000** (= **4 900**); card pulls **20** each (**1 600** per maxed card, **51 200** for
all 32); extra tournament tickets **10 / 20 / 30 …**; **unmerge 20**, **mission reroll 5**; **respecs: Workshop 150,
Bots 300, Guardian 150** (all reducible to **0** by the Vault lines, T017); Event-Shop relics **600 Rare / 1 200 Epic**;
module purchases once tickets run out; guild creation.
⇒ **the ≈ 115 k-gem “framework” bill = 58 400 + 51 200 + 4 900**, i.e. **≈ 1 year of perfect claiming.**

**Power Stones (published prices):** UW unlocks/upgrades; **UW+ 500 for the first → 3 800 for the ninth** (v29.0.5);
**Card Masteries 500–1 250 each = 28 500 for all**; **assist module slots 1 000 each**; **Bot+ 1 250 each**;
**Synchronicity 1 500 to unlock + 3 × 1 500**.
⇒ **the ≈ 60 k-stone bill against ≈ 10.7 k/year ⇒ 5–6 years** ⇒ stones are the binding long-term constraint, which is
why T008 (UW sync) spends them on **uptime first** and the cosmetic/late lines last.
The UI also prints confirmation dialogs at **≥ 200 gems** and **≥ 100 stones**, and **stones are never refunded**.

## 3. Paid ladder (only if you pay at all)

| Rule | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy gems on the Web Store, not in-app** | **+10 % gems** on applicable offers | the official store states it; the in-app price is the same money for 10 % fewer gems |
| **Enter a creator code** | free (no cost to you) | the store pays the creator 5 % (in-app 3.5 %) — nothing changes for your totals |
| **Claim the Web-store Free Gift every week** | free gems | separate from the in-game 20-gem daily claim (H031: does it stack?) |
| **Monthly Stone Packs** (all 3 channels) | stones/year ↑ | the only renewable stone source besides tournaments; at ≈ 10.7 k/yr free, the 60 k bill is otherwise 5–6 years |
| **Milestone Pass (3-tier group)** | 1 505 → 8 930 gem-equivalents | already ranked in T019 §2 — buy right after finishing the group (retroactive) |
| Order of the paid offers | Passes > Monthly Stone Packs > gems > cosmetic themes | passes and stone packs are the only ones whose value is printed; gems convert at the T009 ladder |

The store's own sections are **Featured / Events / Packs / Gems / Stones / Milestone Passes / Free Gift / Support a
Creator** — so “Free Gift” is a first-class section: log in with your **User ID** (profile screen) once a week.

## 4. Steps (UI only)

1. **Daily (1 min):** claim in this order — **Store → Free** (20, or 40 during a holiday sale) → the **bottom-left ad
   gem** icon until it stops paying (60/day) → **Missions** tiles (3 each) → tap **Bob** whenever it is on screen.
2. Keep one **long** autoplay run going overnight so Bob's 10-block cap is reachable (≥30 min + 400 waves between
   taps), instead of only 11-minute auto-restarts.
3. Once the weekly box counter is full, claim the **Weekly Reward** boxes in the **Missions** panel (**185 gems + 45
   stones**).
4. Both tournament days: claim the bracket reward, then spend tickets per T014 (the marginal ticket still pays).
5. **Weekly:** open a browser → `store.techtreegames.com/thetower/` → log in with your **User ID** (in the in-game
   profile) → claim the **Free Gift** section → if paying, use the **Gems** section there (the +10 %), and check
   **Milestone Passes** against T019 §2's ranking first.
6. Convert per T009's order: **lab slots → card slots → `Max Interest` L1–L4**; keep the stone ledger for the §2 list
   (uptime lines first, T008).
7. Do **not** pay 5 gems per mission reroll after the third reroll of a day — one reroll is worth ≈ 0.25 % of a day's
   gem income, three are still cheap, but they are the only “instant” gem sink with no multiplier.

## 5. Reproduction checklist

- [ ] The Store's free offer reads **20** gems (40 during a holiday sale) and the ad icon pays **6**.
- [ ] Bob appears from **wave 10**, and after 10 taps in one run no further block spawns until the run restarts.
- [ ] The Missions panel's **weekly** boxes show **185 gems** total and the 5/6/7 boxes show **10/15/20 stones**.
- [ ] A tournament 1st place in your league prints the §1 table's value (e.g. **Gold = 300 gems / 80 stones**).
- [ ] The web store lists **Milestone Passes** and a **Free Gift** section after a User-ID login, with gems **10 %**
      higher than the in-app offer of the same price.
- [ ] A ≥ 200-gem purchase asks for confirmation, a 199-gem purchase does not (same for 100 stones).

## 6. Falsification

- If the ad icon pays more/less than **6**, the client is pre/post-v29 — rescale the §1 budget by the same factor
  (the 60-claim cap is version-independent).
- If the web-store gem offers match the in-app numbers exactly, the “+10 %” applies only to specific offers → read
  both for one offer and record the per-offer list here (H030).
- If the Weekly Free Gift does not arrive, the login was on a different **User ID** (profile screen) or the claim is
  once per week, not per day — re-read after a full week (H031).
- If the stone ladder in §2 disagrees with the panel (UW+/mastery prices move between versions), trust the panel: the
  *ratio* stones/year : bill (≈ 1 : 6) is the planning number that matters.

## 7. The binary's own ledger (the authoritative taxonomy, 14 ids)

`tools/gm_strings.txt` prints the id list the analytics ledger batches (`batch_currency_analytics` →
`currency_analytics_immediate`, `Log/Currency` → `logCurrencyAudit`), which is the shortest complete answer to
“what counts as a currency”:

| Ledger id | In-game name | Covered by |
|---|---|---|
| `coin` | Coins | T001, T002, T020 |
| `stone` | Power Stones | T008, T022 §2 |
| `keys` | Vault Keys | T017 |
| `medal` | Event/Tournament Medals | T013 |
| `ticket` | Tournament Tickets | T014 |
| `cannon_shard` / `armor_shard` / `generator_shard` / `core_shard` | Module Shards, 4 types | T010 |
| `module_reroll` | Module reroll tokens | T010 |
| `module_ticket` | Module tickets (10 free at T2 W90) | T010 |
| `cells` | Elite Cells | T015, T006 |
| `bits` | Guild Bits | T016 |
| `tokens` | Guild Tokens | T016 |
| — (`gems` / `stones` are separate serialised fields, with a per-item `claimedAt`) | Gems | T007, T009, T012 |

Two details that only the binary gives: **each module type's shards is a separate pool** (so a Cannon-heavy build
cannot spend Armor shards — the reason T010's merge ladder is per-type), and **every claimable carries `claimedAt`**,
i.e. the daily caps in T007/T019 are per-source timestamps, not one shared counter — which is also the structural
argument behind [H031](../hypotheses/H031-webstore-weekly-gift-stacking.md).
The offer-wall/creator UI is tier-gated by a remote-config value `offerWallCreatorCodeGateTier1Wave`, so the TapJoy
offers appear at a fixed Tier-1 wave rather than at a fixed account level.
