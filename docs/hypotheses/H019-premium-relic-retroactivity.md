# H019 — Premium relics (550/1 100) are granted retroactively when the Event Boost is bought mid-event

Status: open · Raised: 2026-11-01

**Claim.** The Event Boost doubles mission milestones **retroactively** (T013 §1: 10/15/20 → 20/30/40 for medals
already earned). The premium relic gates are **550** and **1 100** *in-event mission* medals. If the doubling is
applied to the stored counter, then buying the boost at any time re-grades the counter and both premium relics are
granted immediately once the doubled total passes 550 / 1 100.

**Alternative reading.** The boost only multiplies milestones **claimed after** the purchase, so a mid-event
purchase leaves the already-claimed medals single and the 1 100 gate can go unmet in a short event (the mission
pool caps at 945 single / 1 890 doubled — so with a late boost the 1 100 gate needs nearly every remaining mission).

**Why it matters.** It decides *when* to buy the boost, and the two premium relics are +2 % and +5 % of a stat
(≈ the same as one Rare + one Epic event relic) for a single purchase. Under the retroactive reading the purchase
can be deferred to the end of the event (better information, same result); under the other reading an early
purchase is required or the 1 100 relic is simply lost that event.

**Settling observation (one event).** Record the in-event medal counter three times:
1. after ~12 missions, **before** buying the boost → note `M1` and which gates are lit;
2. buy the **Event Boost**, immediately re-read the same counter → note `M2`;
3. if `M2 ≈ 2 × M1` and the **550** premium relic unlocks at that moment, the retroactive reading holds.
If `M2 = M1` and only future claims are doubled, the early-purchase rule stands.

**Falsifier.** If `M2 = M1` with the boost already active, T013 §1's “retroactively” note needs narrowing to
“milestones claimed after purchase”, and the boost goes first thing on event day 1.
