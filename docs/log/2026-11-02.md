# 2026-11-02 - Session 79 (iteration 88: the seven tournament reward ladders are recorded cell-for-cell and ticket buying collapses to n star = floor of P over 10)

## Asked

§3 carried only the bottom band and the rank-1 row of each league. Question: what do the **complete ten-band tables** add —
is there structure behind the 60–70 printed cells (so the pay of an unlisted band can be derived), and can the ticket price
ladder (`10, 20, 30 …`) be turned into one closed-form “how many tickets should I buy” rule?

## What was established (T014 Rev 8)

**The ladders** (Hub `wiki/system/tournaments`, 2026-11-01, six leagues + fandom's seven with Mythic, dated **v29,
2026-08-26**): all **130 cells** of the Hub tables now sit in §3.2. Cross-source: **20/20 cells identical for Copper,
Silver and Gold** (the leagues a sub-T11 account plays), 6/20 Platinum, 1/20 Champion, 0/20 Legend ⇒ the low leagues are
doubly confirmed and only the upper ones carry the version skew already logged in §3.1. Parsed with the new
**`tools/fandom-rewards-parse.ps1`**, which also confirms **all seven** tables' band widths Σ to **30**.

**Four structure laws.**
1. Band widths are `1,1,2,2,2,2,2,3,7,8` for Copper–Gold and `1,1,2,2,2,2,2,3,9,6` from Platinum up (Σ 30 both) ⇒ above Gold
   the tail is re-cut into 9 + 6 players: **fewer pay steps per place**.
2. The rank-1 columns are separate ladders: gems `100,200,300,400,600,800` (Δ +100 ×3, then +200 ×2) and stones
   `20,40,80,160,320,425` (**×2 four times**, then +105) ⇒ the stone share of a rank-1 ticket climbs **67 % → 84 %** and the
   gems-per-stone ratio collapses 5.00 → 3.75 → 1.88.
3. The ladder flattens on promotion: rank 1 ÷ bottom band = 10× / 5× / 6× then exactly **4×** three times over, so up there
   *league* beats *placement*; concretely **rank 1 in Copper (300 gem-equiv) = bottom-band Platinum (300)** and **rank 1 in
   Gold (1 100) = Champion 16–24 (1 100)**.
4. Σ per bracket: **885 / 1 845 / 3 010 / 5 225 / 7 850 / 12 175** gems and **251 / 525 / 904 / 1 771 / 3 710 / 7 570**
   stones, **137** Legend keys ⇒ per-player means 29.50 → 405.83 gems, and the **mean is above the median band's pay in every
   league** (Copper 29.50 vs 20 ⇒ 1.48×, Legend 405.83 vs 400 ⇒ 1.01×), so a mid-field finish is *below* the bracket average.
   Bonus: the bottom-band **stone floor is flat at 20 from Gold to Champion** ⇒ there, promotion is a gems-only upgrade and
   the stone jump lives in the 16–24 band (50 / 90 / 225).

**The ticket law.** With `P = gems + 10 × stones` for your band, `n` bought tickets cost `5n(n + 1)` and `t = 2 + n` tickets
(free + ad + bought) return `P·t`, so `net(n) = P(n + 2) − 5n(n + 1)` is a concave parabola with `net(n) − net(n − 1) =
P mod 10` ⇒ **buy `n* = floor(P / 10)`** and the return is **≥ ×2.00** (worked: Copper bottom `P = 60` ⇒ 6 tickets, 210 in /
480 out; Gold bottom 25 ⇒ 3 250 in / 6 750 out; Legend rank 1 5 05 ⇒ 1 277 650 in / 2 560 350 out). The stones side is where
it compounds — `t × band stones` per bracket, twice a week: **80/week** at the Copper bottom band vs **6 400/week** at
Platinum 16–24, i.e. **11.4 → 0.14 weeks** to fund the 910-stone GT+DW+BH sync.

**Second ceiling, and the coincidence.** A bracket is 72 h and a tournament run costs 30.5 s per wave (T002), so
`runs ≤ 72 ÷ (30.5 W/3 600)`: **W = 500 ⇒ ≈ 17**, **W = 1 000 ⇒ 8**, **W = 2 000 ⇒ 4**. For the target reader the gem optimum
(8 tickets) and the clock optimum (8 runs of wave 1 000) **meet** — “6 bought tickets + free + ad, ≈ wave 1 000 each” is both.
