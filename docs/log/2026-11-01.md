## What was established

**T011 — Tune the perk engine.** The number of perk picks in a run is a *formula*, not a constant:
`N = floor((Base − WavesRequiredLab) × (1 − PWRqty × 0.20 × (1 + SPB/100)))`, with `Base` stepping

exactly: 5 picks per 1 000 waves untuned vs **16** with `Waves Required` L20 + 3× PWR + SPB 10 (47 w/pick at
L30/SPB 20). Plus three mechanics that are easy to miss: the PWR perk is **retroactive**, unpicked perks
**queue**, and the offer menu **cannot be rerolled** — so `Perk Option Quantity` + `Ban Perks` are the only
quality levers. Offer-slot roll: 68 % standard / 20 % UW / 12 % trade-off (85/15 when no UW is possible) ⇒
P(≥1 standard) rises 89.8 % → 99.0 % going from 2 to 4 slots.

(displayed 6.3). Fandom notes the nominal multiplier overstates real throughput (x5.0 ≈ x4.0 in practice).

# 2026-11-01 — Session 3 (iteration 12: Elite Cells vs Gems)

the existing T006 rather than creating a duplicate entry.

## What was established (T006 Rev 2)

Boost prices are **flat per hour per slot** since v25.5, which makes the efficiency ranking exact:

| ×1.5 | ×2 | ×3 | ×4 | ×5 | ×6 | ×7 | ×8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 100 | 420 | 1 120 | 2 975 | 12 000 | 41 667 | 142 857 |

(cells per **skipped** hour; each step ≈ 3.3× worse). So: ×1.5 on all five slots = 75 cells/h = 1 800/day,
and escalating one slot past ×3 is only rational with a six-figure cell stock.

**Verdict on cells vs gems:** 1 skipped research-hour costs **30 cells** or **2.89–8 gems** depending on the
gem bucket (1 h = 8, 1 d = 6.79, 1 w = 5.95, 30 d = 4.93, 90 d = 3.70, 360 d = 2.89 gems/h). Against a
sub-T11 income of ≈ 40 gems/day and ≈ 1 000 cells/day, a cell-bought hour consumes ~3 % of the day's cells
while a gem-bought hour consumes ~17 % of the day's gems ⇒ **cells first, always; gems only for the multi-day
gate labs, bought in the largest bucket**, and gold-box the labs first (+0.015× rush efficiency each).

Two previously unnoticed cell-flow facts: the **Wave Skip** card is cell-positive (**1.1×** the current wave's
cells per skipped wave) while **Intro Sprint is cell-negative (20 % of normal)** — so an Intro-Sprint-started
run is the wrong run for a cell-farming day. Elite = Vampire / Ray / Scatter, drop `1 … Tier`, average
`(1 + Tier)/2` for Tiers 1–13.

# 2026-11-01 — Session 4 (iteration 13: Cards)

## Asked

New angle, not yet covered: the **Cards** system as a *currency* engine (pull economics, which levels to
buy, which cards pay for themselves).

## What was established (T012 new, T009 Rev 2)

**Pool model.** 32 cards = 12 Common / 9 Rare / 11 Epic; a pull is 20 gems (`Buy x10` = 200) and rolls
**80 / 17 / 3** rarity first, then a card inside it. Level 7 leaves the pool and an exhausted rarity's share
migrates to the others ⇒ the full deck is **2 560 pulls = 51 200 gems ≈ 4 years at ~40 gems/day** (the
game-vault figures 2 400 / 48 000 / 19 slots are the older 30-card pool — recorded as a version gap).

**The decision rule that makes the technique useful.** Copies per level-up are fixed
(2 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 20 / 32 = 40 / 100 / 160 / 240 / 400 / 640 gems) but the percentage points differ by card,
so every purchase can be compared in **gems per +1 pt**: Cash 2 → 32, Enemy Balance 4 → 64, Coins 8 → 128 as
the card goes L1→L7. Since **card slot 7 costs 500 gems and delivers a whole extra card**, the optimum is
*L1–L4 on every currency card, then buy slots, then return for L5–7*. That numeric tie-breaker is what T009's
“alternate slots and labs” was missing, so it went in as T009 Rev 2 rather than a new entry.

**Currency-first card set** with their L1→L7 values: Enemy Balance (cash/kill ×1.30→×1.90 + 80 % double
spawns), Cash (×1.20→×2.40), Coins (×1.15→×1.45), Critical Coin (15 %→33 % of basics carry +1 coin),
Wave Skip (9 %→19 %, and each skip pays wave coins + wave cash + **interest** + 1.1× the current wave's
coins/cash/**cells** — the only card that pays interest twice), Cells (+2→+14 flat cells per elite, applied
after multipliers, so it is worth most at *low* tiers).

**UI facts harvested:** the Cards ℹ panel lists live per-rarity and per-card chances; **hold** a card for all 7
level values; **tap** equips into the first empty slot; 10 cards are locked mid-round and nothing can change
while a boss/fleet enemy is alive; `Card Presets` (T1 W400) = 5 presets, and mid-run switching only works if
the locked-card sets match ⇒ keep the locked cards identical across presets. Milestone cards only enter the
pool **after** the milestone is claimed: Recovery Package Chance T2 W750, **Cells T4 W40**, Land Mine Stun
T7 W250, Nuke T11 W10, Ultimate Crit T14 W50, Area of Effect T20 W80.

**Masteries are out of reach below Tier 16** (T16 W100 milestone *and* 30 cards at L7, then 500–1 250 stones
each, 28 500 total, plus 9-level labs at 1.10 q→10.00 q coins). Practical consequence for a sub-T11 account:
**do not hoard Stones** — spend them on the UW sync (T008). Also recorded for later: the money masteries are
`Coins+` ×1.05…×1.32 (via the Cells card's sibling), **Double Coins** 10 %→100 %, **Elite Farming** 0.4 %→4 %
reroll-shard chance per elite kill, **Elite Balance** 6 %→60 % (the unknown in H009).

# 2026-11-01 — Session 5 (iteration 14: Modules)

## Asked

New angle: the **Module** economy (shards, slot levels, merge recipes, reroll shards) — the system T010 Rev 1
covered only superficially.

## Verdict: Rev 1 was wrong in three places, now corrected in T010 Rev 2

1. **Levels belong to the slot, not to the module.** Each of the 4 slots (and each assist slot) levels to 300
   with its own shard type + coins; the equipped module is “Level Capped by Rarity”. Swapping is free of
   loss and an **empty slot can be levelled in advance** — which is the whole trick, and it inverts Rev 1's
   advice about leveling commons.
2. **Common modules cannot be merged at all.** The ladder starts at Rare (Rare → Legendary+, natural Epic →
   Ancestral 5★). Ancestral stars: each needs an Epic+ of the same module, +20 max level, main effect
   ×1.04…×1.20.
3. **Merged Rares never get a Unique Effect** and keep their old name ⇒ they are wildcard material only.

## Numbers harvested

- **Slot cost milestones** (cumulative shards / coins): 41 = **1 715 / 7.45 M**, 101 = 23 595 / 16.3 B,
  141 = 91 595 / 1.31 T, 161 = 163 595 / 20.81 q, 201 = 466 095 / 41.91 q, 241 = 1 071 095 / 14.46 Q,
  300 = 3 136 095 / 18.07 s. Shard price per level grows near-linearly (7 → 49 500) while coins explode, so
  below ~level 101 the **coins** are the bottleneck, above it the **shards**.
- **Sub-stat unlocks at slot levels 41 / 101 / 141 / 161 / 201 / 241**, and the **arrow** beside the locked
  sub-stat levels the slot straight there in one tap — the cheapest multiplier-per-shard in the system is
  reaching **41 on all four slots first**.
- **Shatter ladder:** Common 5 / Rare 10 / Rare+ 30 / merged Epic 90, **natural Epic 1 000** (only while still
  Epic and only once a 5★ Ancestral of that module is owned); `Shatter Shards` lab +20 %/level (max +100 %).
- **Derived, and useful:** *unmerging is shard-neutral* — 3 × Rare+ = 90 shards either shattered directly or
  routed through merge→unmerge (Epic refunds exactly 2 × 30 = 60), so the **20-gem** unmerge fee is really the
  price of a **sub-stat reroll** without spending reroll shards (returned modules get new sub-stats). The same
  2× structure holds at 360 and 720 in the unmerge table, which also lets the missing shatter values be
  inferred: Epic+ ≈ 180, Legendary+ = 360.
- **Reroll shards:** 15 % per boss kill, tier-scaled amount (**T5 = 12, T10 = 35, T11 = 40**, T24 = 105),
  +1 per `Reroll Shards` lab level, and a **5-wave cooldown after any drop** ⇒ the income is capped per wave,
  not per boss. Lock cost ladder **10 / 40 / 160 / 500 / 1 000 / 1 600 / 2 250 / 3 000** for 0–7 locks ⇒ a
  **300× escalation**, so ≤3 locks and use **Auto Roll** with a narrow acceptance list; the ban labs
  (Cannon 4, Armor 4, Generator 3, Core 7) shrink the pool instead.
- Sub-stat rarity odds 61.2 / 25 / 10 / 2.5 / 1.0 / 0.3 %, capped by the module's own rarity ⇒ merging is what
  unlocks Ancestral sub-stats at all.
- **Restore** is free, returns all shards+coins, keeps sub-stats, does **not** return reroll shards. Favourite/
  equipped/preset-saved modules cannot be shattered. Inventory cap 350.
- Pull side: 10 **free tickets** at unlock (tickets consumed before gems, first Epic guaranteed in the 10),
  banner rates 68.5 / 29 / 2.5 %, **pity at pull 150**, every 10-pull guarantees ≥1 Rare.
- Assist stage recorded for later: 1 000 stones/slot, uniques 4 600, main effect 16 038, substat efficiency
  8 073; quantity sub-stats use **FLOOR** (34 % needed for a +3-quantity line); assists became multiplicative
  for all types in v27.1.

# 2026-11-01 — Session 6 (iteration 15: Events & Medals)

## Asked

New angle: the **Medal / Event Shop** economy — listed as unexplored in session 1 (including the question
whether the 150-gem trade is worth it).

## What was established (T013 new, H013, H002 corroborated)

**Income.** 21 missions × 45 medals (10/15/20) = **945**, plus weekly daily-mission boxes **135/week** (270), plus
Guardian **Fetch** ≤10/day ⇒ **≈ 1 215 F2P medals per 2-week event** (~87/day). The Event Boost doubles the
milestones and does so **retroactively**.

**The gate rule.** The relic gates (**350** Rare / **700** Epic) are fed **only by event-mission medals** —
carried-over medals and weekly-box medals do not count. Since the mission pool caps at 945, the Epic relic needs
≈ 16 of 21 missions: missions first, boxes later. → **H013** (one claim-before-missions test settles it).

**The shop ladder is the real find.** Per-line, per-event counters, resetting when a new event starts:
**40 / 65 / 90 / 130 / 175 / 230 / 380** medals for 150 gems **or** 15 stones **or** 250 shards. Efficiency drops
2.4× from trade 1 to trade 4 (gems: 3.75 → 1.15 per medal). Therefore:
- take **trades 1–3 on all three lines = 585 medals → 450 gems + 45 stones + 750 shards** (vs 1 110 medals for
  1 050 gems if you grind one line to 7);
- because the counters **reset every event**, banked medals are not idle — banked medals + a fresh event buy
  trade-1 prices again, which is why keeping **500–700** in reserve beats spending them late in a shop.
- **Answering session 1's open question:** the 150-gem-for-40-medal trade is *the best single conversion in the
  shop* (3.75 gems/medal at trade 1) and beats the old “150-gem event shop is not recommended” note, which only
  applies once the line's counter is past ~4.

**Double-dip scheduling** (the technique's core): the missions are the currency actions themselves — the T007
ad sweep alone advances three missions (**135 medals for taps you already do**), the “Lab research time” mission
counts **gem-rushed** time (so T006's rush buckets belong inside an event), and the card/workshop missions
capture T012's pulls and T002's upgrades.

**Bot ladder:** 150 / 300 / 600 / 900 / 1 200 medals (cumulative 150 → 3 150), and bots are **respec-able once
per event for free** ⇒ surplus medals are a refundable store of value.

Also: the event-mission table repeats the `max tier − 2` offset on two mission families → recorded as a
corroboration in **H002** (both wikis may still be quoting the same tooltip).

## Verdict

T013 **Unverified**. Cheapest numeric capture: the medal counter after one mission (45), the first two gem trades
(40 then 65), and the relic counter after claiming a weekly box (H013).

# 2026-11-01 — Session 7 (iteration 16: Tournaments)

## Asked

## What was established (T014 new, T013 Rev 2, H014)

**Cadence & mechanics:** brackets of **30 players** every **Wednesday and Saturday 00:00 UTC**; 1 ticket = 1 run,
best wave scored; **1 free ticket + 1 from an ad**, then `10 × n` gems for the n-th extra; **top 4 promote**, bottom
6 demote only from Platinum up, and **Copper/Silver/Gold are protected** ⇒ promotion below Platinum is a one-way
ratchet. Joining needs the run to be finished (and the join is a server call: `joinTournamentV5`).

**The repository's shared unit.** The Event Shop's 40-medal trades fix the internal rates, now written once in
T014 §2 and referenced from T013 Rev 2: **1 Stone = 10 gems · 1 Module Shard = 0.6 gems · 1 Medal = 0.267 gems**.

**Bottom-band ticket value (gem-equivalents):** Copper **60** · Silver **160** · Gold **250** · Platinum **300** ·
Champion **375** · Legend **1 250** (keys start here) · Mythic **2 585**. With the 10n ticket price the marginal
value of the n-th extra ticket is `reward − 10n`: Copper +50/+40/+30/+20/+10/0, Gold +240…+190. ⇒ **1–3 extras in
Copper, 3–5 from Gold upward**, always compared against the next card slot (50–10 000).

**Tournament-specific facts that change the preset:** no **perks** in tournaments (so optimise waves, not coins/min);
Heat ramps to full strength at **wave 1 000**; the **Overheat boss interval is static per league — 10/9/8/7/6/5
waves** for Copper→Legend, which is also the cleanest support for **H012**'s assumption that the normal interval
is 10; **Enemy Level Skip** is fixed at 10/20/30/50 % for Gold/Platinum/Champion/Legend; **Death Defy or Energy
Shield** is active from Platinum up; **ties pay the lowest rank in the tied group**, so overtaking by one wave is
worth a whole reward step.

**Reward detail recorded:** rank-1 payouts 100/20 (Copper) → 300/80 (Gold) → 425/180 (Platinum) → 550/300
(Champion) → 675/375/25 keys (Legend) → 900/475/50 keys (Mythic, added v29 on 26 Aug 2026).

## What was established (T015 new, H015)

rises +40 per level**, so an L-level line costs `100L + 20L(L−1)` → 15 lv = **5 700**, 20 lv = 9 600,
30 lv = **20 400** medals. Against ~1 215 medals/event that is **17 events for one 30-level line**, and a fully
levelled Golden Bot (30+15+30+20) is **56 100 medals ≈ 46 events**. Bots are a multi-year sink, so the order of
lines matters more than the medal total — and the free once-per-event respec makes early levels risk-free.

**Stat lines captured for all five bots** (Golden ×2.0→×8.0 coin bonus / 20→35 s / 120→75 s / 20→60 m;
Amplify ×3.5→×15.5; Flame ×50→×290 damage and 20 %→95 % damage reduction, cooldown 75→30 s; Thunder stun +
20 % linger (½ on bosses/elites); Bot Bot multiplies the others).

**The uptime arithmetic (why Cooldown comes first):** 120 s → 30 activations/h at 16.7 % uptime; medals-maxed
75 s → **48/h at 46.7 %**; with the cooldown lab 50 s/45 s window → **72/h at 90 % uptime**. The 15-level
cooldown line (5 700 medals) is the cheapest large multiplier on any bot.

**The compounding chain:** Golden Bot bonus ×8 × Bot Bot multiplier × Golden Tower window = coins; and on the
cell side **Golden Bot+ “Bonus Cells” ×1.25→×2.50** inside the window, then **Bot Bot+ “Maximum Power”
×1.25→×2.50** on top → potentially **×6.25 Elite Cells**, which converts straight into lab-boost hours
(T006: 30 cells per skipped hour). Bot+ gates behind owning all five bots and costs **1 250 stones** each, with
its own +50-medal-per-level ladder (totals 17 500 / 11 500 / 11 500 / 11 500 / 8 100 = 60 100).
